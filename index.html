Who am I?

I’m Michaela — a software engineering student with a passion for solving real-world problems through technology. My interests lie in data science, cybersecurity, and digital transformation, especially in the healthcare space where I’m building a paperless clinical record system to reduce waste and improve care in my country.

Outside of code, I’m a lifelong creative — from crocheting and sewing to performing arts. I’ve worked with the National Institute of Allied Arts in Zimbabwe as a musical performer and now support them as an auxiliary staff member.

I’m also a student ambassador and current sStudent Ambassador Secretary General, where I lead, mentor, and grow alongside fellow student leaders. I’m cheerful, bilingual (English and Shona), currently learning French and Portuguese and always up for a challenge.

What can I do?

I build, I lead, and I create — across both tech and the arts.

As I work toward a career in data-driven, secure systems, I’m also filling my GitHub with mini projects that reflect my learning, curiosity, and technical range. My coding toolkit includes HTML, CSS, Python, and JavaScript — and I’m expanding into data analysis, cybersecurity, and systems design.

I’m especially passionate about building tech for good — from eco-conscious systems to community-centered platforms. Whether it’s designing intuitive interfaces or bringing structure to complex problems, I approach every project with a mix of creativity, logic, and a desire to make a difference.
